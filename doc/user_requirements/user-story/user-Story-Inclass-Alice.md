## Who is the user
Alice is a neuroscience researcher who studies how the brain controls movement and speech. She understands research theory well but isn’t comfortable with coding or data processing. She prefers tools that are easy to use and don’t require technical setup.

## What she wants to do
Alice wants to compare brain activity between human speech attempts and monkey reaching movements. She wants to upload data, view activity across trials, and see whether similar patterns appear in both species.

## What she needs from the tool
- A simple way to load both datasets  
- Automatic processing of the human speech data so it matches the monkey data structure  
- Clear visualizations comparing neural activity between tasks  
- Easy explanations of results  
- Export of figures and summaries for discussion

## Skill level
Alice understands neuroscience well and can interpret scientific results. However, she prefers click-based workflows instead of command-line coding. The tool should guide her step by step and use clear visuals without technical jargon.
