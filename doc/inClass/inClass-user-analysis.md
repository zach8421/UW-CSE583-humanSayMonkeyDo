# Format

For each user persona, please provide the following information:

- Who is the user.
- What do they want to do with the tool.
- What needs and desires do they want for the tool.
- What is their skill level.

Alice is a researcher. She wants to use this tool to discover whether who research areas are duplicable. She has plenty of domain knowledge but has no expertise in technical stuff so she prefers a simple interface and user-friendly functionality design.
